# ALISIO business audit — redesign direction

## First design plan

- Keep the ALISIO lime mark, precise operational language, and warm coral exception color.
- Rebuild the page as an editorial business story: promise, visible workflow, human control, tangible audit outputs, then a short application.
- Add a premium, cinematic team image and a small set of supporting images that show people working with their existing systems.
- Add dimensional motion to process lines, image framing, and section transitions.

## Critique pass 1

- A mostly black page repeats the visual weight of the old version and can read as an empty backdrop rather than a confident brand.
- An abstract diagram alone does not give the owner an immediate human reason to trust the audit.
- Many moving cards and routes would compete with the offer and make the page feel like an AI demo.

### Revision

- Use warm off-white as the main canvas, deep pine/graphite as framing surfaces, lime only for action and progress, coral only for exceptions.
- Put a real team in the first viewport; keep the X-ray as an explanatory visual, with no fake dashboard copy or made-up metrics.
- Use fewer motion moments: an image reveal, a traced workflow, and modest scroll entry; disable them for reduced-motion settings.

## Critique pass 2

- A light-first redesign risks losing the recognizable ALISIO/night-operations signature if lime and dark sections disappear.
- A large cinematic image can push the CTA below the fold and crop poorly on a phone.
- A detailed audit form can undermine conversion if every detail is required before a first conversation.
- Animation must not obscure copy, hide controls, or depend on scroll position.

## Final build plan

1. Use a light, high-contrast hero with the ALISIO wordmark, explicit no-replacement promise, clear primary CTA, and responsive image crop.
2. Alternate light and deep graphite sections so the brand remains recognizable without a long black void.
3. Explain one workflow from intake to human-approved action, with a clear interaction that works by keyboard and touch.
4. Present outcomes and prioritization with restrained motion and no invented ROI, testimonials, or replacement claims.
5. Keep the application concise, consent explicit, and WhatsApp delivery as an unsent draft that the visitor reviews and sends themselves.
6. Respect reduced motion, focus visibility, contrast, and mobile layout; critique the rendered page once, then make a final polish pass.
